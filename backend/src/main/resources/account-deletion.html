Delete your LedgerNest account

You can permanently delete your account here without installing the app. Verify your identity using your current sign-in identifier and password.

This deletes your profile, sign-in identifier, debts, repayments, businesses, business transactions, recovery contacts, verification challenges and linked identifier history from the live application database. Deletion cannot be undone. Any existing sessions lose access.

Hosting backups and provider logs follow the operator’s retention policies; they are not erased by this action. See LedgerNest’s published privacy policy for retention details.
